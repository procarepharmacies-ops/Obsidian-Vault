# ProCare Pharmacy Brand Guidelines

## Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** Expert pharmaceutical care that treats every patient like family.
- **Vision:** To lead the future of community health by nurturing a family-oriented healthcare network.

## Brand Colors
- **ProBlue:** `#0c72bc` (Primary - Trust, medical authority, stability)
- **ProGreen:** `#8BC34A` (Accent - Wellness, vitality, organic growth)
- **Clinical White:** `#FFFFFF` (Background - Clean, high-end professional feel)

## Typography
- **Headings:** Montserrat Bold (60pt Title, 32pt Heading, 24pt Subtitle)
- **Body:** Open Sans Regular (16pt Section Header, 12pt Body)

## Design Rules
- **Flat Design:** 2D flat vectors, 2pt consistent monoline stroke with rounded caps.
- **The ProCare Filter:** 15% ProBlue tint overlay on photography.
- **Animations:** Gentle "Pulse" effect for vitality elements, clockwise rotation for progress elements. Smooth "Ease-in-out" transitions.
